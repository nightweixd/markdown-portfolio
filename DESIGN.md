# BSC Radar

A personal trading workbench: discovery and comparison are the primary tasks.
The local design search matched data-dense dashboards; its marketing gateway
layout did not fit, so it is not used.

Palette: midnight #121420, panel #1B1E2C, divider #303447, text #F1F2F7,
BNB gold #F3C758, positive mint #69D9B4. Violet #ABA1F5 marks selection.
Type: Segoe UI / system sans for readable controls and tabular figures for data.
No decorative fake charts or market-wide totals. Motion is limited to feedback.

Layout: a narrow navigation rail, broad filterable market table, contextual
token detail panel. Headers and figures align to the table. The panel moves below
the table on tablets and opens as a separate section on phones.

Risk flags show reported facts and unknown data separately. No safety score is
invented. Source coverage is visible and fictional fixtures are opt-in only.
